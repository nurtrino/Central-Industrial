Privacy policy

Home Deliveries poller. Effective 18 November 2026.

Who this covers

Home Deliveries poller is a private tool used by one household. The only Google account it connects to is a mailbox that the household created for this purpose. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What Google data it reads

With the account owner's consent, the tool has read-only access to that Gmail mailbox (the gmail.readonly permission). It reads shipping and delivery notices: the sender, subject, date and text of those emails. It cannot send, change or delete mail.

How the data is used

The emails are used for one thing: working out which packages are expected and when, so the home's automation system can announce arrivals to the household. To do that, the text of a shipping notice is processed by an AI service acting only on the household's behalf. The data is not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Where it is kept

The list of expected packages is kept on a computer inside the home. Orders drop off that list a few days after delivery, or after three weeks without an update. The emails themselves stay in the mailbox.

Sharing

The data is not sold and not shared with anyone outside the household, apart from the processing described above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account owner can end the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
